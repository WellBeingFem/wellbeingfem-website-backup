# WellBeingFem Step 1 — Ground-Truth Visual Specification

This is a **reconstruction task**, not a redesign. The uploaded cream-background logo, finished homepage hero, and 2026 branding card are the authoritative references. Their existing identity, imagery, composition, proportions, colours, and wording must be preserved.

## Chosen Design Philosophy

**Faithful WellBeingFem restoration:** an elegant, calm, spacious, refined, and quietly luxurious presentation built directly around the existing cream, gold, green, and serif-led visual language. No substitute branding, unrelated colour, gradient, font, imagery, decoration, or modernisation may be introduced.

## Design Movement

Soft heritage wellness branding with restrained editorial elegance.

## Core Principles

1. Preserve the supplied assets without cropping, distortion, alteration, or reinterpretation.
2. Use cream as the continuous environmental background, gold as the fine accent, and established green for navigation and interaction states.
3. Maintain generous spacing, balanced proportions, and understated detail.
4. Keep Step 1 structurally limited to the global styling, header/navigation, logo, hero, and transparent hero link.

## Colour Philosophy

The website uses the branding card’s warm cream to create calm and continuity, muted WellBeingFem gold to express refinement without glare, and the established botanical green to provide grounded contrast. Dark serif text carries authority while remaining soft. No unrelated colours or gradients are permitted.

## Layout Paradigm

A quiet horizontal header sits above one complete, centred hero image. Desktop navigation is linear with restrained dropdown panels; mobile navigation expands vertically. No content section follows the hero in Step 1.

## Signature Elements

The supplied oval cream-background logo, fine gold rules, and precise green-and-cream menu treatment are the only recurring brand motifs required at this stage.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions are discreet and functional. Dropdowns reveal quickly without theatrical effects, focus treatment remains accessible, the mobile menu expands clearly, and the embedded hero button is activated by one invisible link aligned to the artwork.

## Animation

Only short opacity-and-transform transitions under 220ms are permitted for dropdowns and the mobile menu. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. The logo and hero remain still.

## Typography System

Headings and navigation use a classic dark serif aligned with the branding card’s editorial character. Supporting interface copy, where required for accessibility, uses a complementary readable serif/sans treatment without competing with the supplied artwork. No branding wordmark is recreated in type.

## Brand Essence

**Positioning:** restorative wellbeing for women, presented with calm authority and established visual refinement. **Personality:** serene, feminine, considered.

## Brand Voice

Navigation and microcopy are concise, gentle, and direct. Examples: “Explore Healy” and “Open Research”. Generic filler language is not used.

## Wordmark & Logo

Use the supplied WellBeingFem cream-background logo directly, at its original proportions. Do not reconstruct the wordmark, isolate its elements, or place it on a white tile.

## Signature Brand Colour

**WellBeingFem gold**, used as a measured accent against the continuous cream ground.

## Asset Rules

The header uses `WellBeingfemlogofinal.png`. The homepage hero uses `NewHeroAug13.png`. `WBFBranding2026.png` is a visual reference for the global colour, typography, spacing, and button language only and is not inserted as an additional page section.

## Scope Boundary

Do not build homepage content below the hero, service cards, service pages, Research content, a footer, forms, or any other section. The `/research` route exists only as the exact target for navigation and the hero’s transparent clickable area; it must not contain Research content during Step 1.
